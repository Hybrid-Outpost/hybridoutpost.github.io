# Welcome to Hybrid Outpost

Hybrid Outpost LLC is an independent studio and consultancy based in Brooklyn, New York. We work at the intersection of product strategy, travel, and independent media — building things that are useful, durable, and worth caring about.

The name is the thesis. An outpost is small by design: self-sufficient, far from headquarters, close to the ground. Hybrid because the work deliberately crosses disciplines — enterprise technology and cultural writing, product management and travel planning, code and editorial. The interesting problems live in the seams between fields, and we've built a practice around working there.

Learn more [about the studio](/about/), or [get in touch](/contact/).
